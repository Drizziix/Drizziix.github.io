T12Trace the chain

Your pinky going numb can start at your neck.

Here's how I think about it. Start where it hurts and follow the nerve back. Tap any point on the chain.

01 · Where you feel it

The ulnar nerve's territory

The pinky and the pinky side of the ring finger get their feeling from the ulnar nerve. So numbness there is a clue about which wire to follow, not proof of where the problem is.

Education only, not medical advice. It's not the same for everyone. If something's numb or weak, see a licensed provider. Have any questions? Let me show you more.
